## Jou werk behoort aan jou

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Plaaslik as verstek" icon="laptop">
    Keep working without an internet connection. Your documents live on your device unless you choose to save them somewhere else.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Gebruik jou eie wolk" icon="rocket">
    Koppel WebDAV-dienste soos Nextcloud om jou notas oral te sinkroniseer, te rugsteun en te wysig.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Oopbronsagteware" icon="code-branch">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Skep saam" icon="comment">

</CardGrid>
