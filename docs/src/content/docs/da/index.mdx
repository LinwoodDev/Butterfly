  <Card title="Alle elementer kan stadig redigeres" icon="pencil">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Alle gængse medier i ét dokument" icon="document">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Værktøjer til enhver idé" icon="open-book">

  Start with a template that brings together your favorite backgrounds, tools, layers, areas, and pages. Save reusable content in packs so your next project starts with everything you need.

## Dit arbejde tilhører dig

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Lokalt som standard" icon="laptop">
    Keep working without an internet connection. Your documents live on your device unless you choose to save them somewhere else.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Brug din egen cloud" icon="rocket">
    Forbind WebDAV-tjenester som Nextcloud for at synkronisere, sikkerhedskopiere og redigere dine noter, uanset hvor du er.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Open source-software" icon="code-branch">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Skab sammen" icon="comment">
    Den eksperimentelle samarbejdsfunktion understøtter end-to-end-krypterede sessioner via Swamp eller en WebSocket-server, du selv hoster.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
