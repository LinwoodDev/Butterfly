## Powerful when your projects grow

<CardGrid stagger>
  <Card title="Every element stays editable" icon="pencil">

  </Card>
  <Card title="常見媒體，集中於一份文件" icon="document">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Reusable building blocks" icon="puzzle">

  </Card>
  <Card title="每個想法都有合適工具" icon="open-book">

  </Card>

  Start with a template that brings together your favorite backgrounds, tools, layers, areas, and pages. Save reusable content in packs so your next project starts with everything you need.
</LandingFeature>

## 你的作品屬於你

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Local by default" icon="laptop">
    Keep working without an internet connection. Your documents live on your device unless you choose to save them somewhere else.
  </Card>
  <Card title="使用自己的雲端" icon="rocket">
    連接 Nextcloud 等 WebDAV 服務，隨時隨地同步、備份與編輯筆記。
  </Card>
  <Card title="開放原始碼軟體" icon="code-branch">

  </Card>
  <Card title="一起創作" icon="comment">

</CardGrid>
