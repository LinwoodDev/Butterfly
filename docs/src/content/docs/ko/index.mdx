  Draw with a stylus, touch, or mouse. Adjust stroke width, color, smoothing, and texture. If a mark is not quite right, edit it instead of starting over.
</LandingFeature>

## Powerful when your projects grow

<CardGrid stagger>
  <Card title="Every element stays editable" icon="pencil">
    Move, resize, recolor, and reorganize content after placing it. Your first attempt never has to be your final one.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Common media in one document" icon="document">
    Import camera photos, gallery images, PDFs, and SVGs. Combine them with your own notes and export clean results in common formats.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reusable building blocks" icon="puzzle">
    Turn favorite layouts into templates and frequently used content into packs, stamps, palettes, and styles available across documents.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tools for every idea" icon="open-book">
    Go beyond pen and eraser with labels, shapes, polygons, rulers, grids, textures, stamps, a laser pointer, presentation tools, and more.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

<LandingFeature image={collageLight} darkImage={collageDark} alt="Butterfly templates and packs with reusable canvas styles">




  ## Make Butterfly fit your workflow

  Start with a template that brings together your favorite backgrounds, tools, layers, areas, and pages. Save reusable content in packs so your next project starts with everything you need.
</LandingFeature>

<LandingFeature image={crossplatform} darkImage={crossplatformDark} alt="A handwritten Butterfly document on a phone and on a desktop" reverse>





  ## Works everywhere

  Take your canvas from your desk to your next class or meeting. Butterfly runs on Android, Windows, macOS, Linux, and in your browser, with an iOS preview also available.

</LandingFeature>

## Your work belongs to you

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Local by default" icon="laptop">
    Keep working without an internet connection. Your documents live on your device unless you choose to save them somewhere else.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bring your own cloud" icon="rocket">
    Connect WebDAV services like Nextcloud to sync, back up, and edit your notes anywhere.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Open source software" icon="code-branch">
    Butterfly is free and open source under the GNU AGPL-3.0 license. Keep your documents in its documented `.bfly` and `.tbfly` formats.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Create together" icon="comment">
    Experimental collaboration supports end-to-end encrypted sessions through Swamp or a WebSocket server you host yourself.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
